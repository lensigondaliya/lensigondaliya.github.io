Audience Choice for Best Presentation

Enhancing Content Discovery on Streaming Platforms

Undergraduate research with Dawens Fortune, mentored by Dr. Poonam Kumari. Student Arts & Research Symposium, Bridgewater State University, April 2026.

An exploratory analysis of an 85,855-record, 22-attribute IMDb dataset to find what drives a film’s popularity. Vote count and review volume came out as the strongest predictors, and a Flask web app serves the trained classifier for interactive predictions.
